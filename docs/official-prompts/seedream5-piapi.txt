Overview
#
Seedream 5.0 provides high-quality image generation from text prompts, with optional image references for editing. Tasks are delivered synchronously (no polling required).

Parameters
#
size
#
Controls output resolution.
Lite models:
2K
(default) or
3K
.
Pro models:
1K
(default) or
2K
. Pro 2K renders at roughly twice the pixel dimensions of 1K (e.g. 16:9 → 2560x1440 vs 1312x736).
aspect_ratio
#
Supported ratios:
1:1
,
16:9
,
9:16
,
4:3
,
3:4
,
3:2
,
2:3
,
4:5
,
5:4
,
21:9
. Default:
1:1
.
The upstream API does not accept an
aspect_ratio
field directly; this engine encodes the requested ratio into the pixel dimensions sent upstream.
output_format
#
Output image format. Accepted values:
jpeg
(default),
png
,
webp
.
image_urls
#
Optional. Provide up to 10 image URLs to use as references for editing / style transfer. Use publicly accessible URLs (CDN or cloud storage); signed/expiring URLs may fail.
For Pro models, the first reference image is free and each additional image adds $0.003 (see Pricing).
sequential_image_generation (advanced, Lite only)
#
disabled
(default) or
auto
. When
auto
, supply
max_images
(1–15) to control batch size; the model will return multiple related images in a single request.
Not supported on Pro models
— these fields are ignored for
seedream-5-pro
and
seedream-5-pro-less-restriction
.
Content moderation
#
All models apply content moderation. Submissions found to contain disallowed content are rejected and your credits are refunded.
Strict models
apply our standard content review.
-less-restriction
models
apply a more permissive content review. A rejection is final and is not retried — your credits are refunded and the task ends with a content-violation error.
Response
#
Seedream is delivered synchronously. The response contains the generated image URLs directly — no polling is required.
Tips
#
Use publicly accessible URLs for any reference images.
For higher resolutions (Lite 3K, Pro 2K), expect slightly longer generation times.
The output is one or more images (not video).
